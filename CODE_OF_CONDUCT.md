# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We expect contributors and maintainers to keep project discussions respectful,
technical, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and implementation tradeoffs.
- Keep feedback specific, actionable, and evidence-based.
- Avoid sharing private papers, confidential review content, or personal data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private or confidential material without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation
for behavior that undermines a safe and productive project environment.
